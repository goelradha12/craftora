Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we get most often.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 5–7 business days. Express shipping is available at checkout for 2–3 day delivery.

Can I place a bulk or corporate order?

Absolutely. We offer volume discounts starting at 20 units. Drop us a message with your requirements and we'll send a custom quote.

What's your return policy?

Customized products are non-returnable unless there's a defect on our end. Standard products can be returned within 7 days of delivery.

Can I upload my own design?

Not yet - but we're working on it! Contact us directly for custom orders.

What's the minimum order quantity?

1 unit. We offer volume discounts starting at 20 units. Drop us a message with your requirements and we'll send a custom quote.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently we ship within India only. International shipping is on our roadmap — sign up to be notified when it launches.
